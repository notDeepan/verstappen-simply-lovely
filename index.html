AN INDEPENDENT DESIGN STUDY

MADE OF RACING.

A cinematic fan-made redesign exploring Max Verstappen’s story. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Max Verstappen, Red Bull Racing or Formula 1.

Imagery & motion

Racing stills and footage come from the supplied fan edits. They illustrate the atmosphere, not necessarily the specific races described. The opening uses the supplied racing film as a silent looping background. An earlier GPT Image portrait is retained in the source archive. The helmet chapter features bad_bovy’s detailed 2024 Imola Schuberth SF3 ABP model, presented through its public Sketchfab embed with the creator’s matching preview. View the original model and credits ↗. The earlier invented helmet concept has been retired.

The record

Historical milestones are based on Formula 1’s published reports. Championship figures refer to 2021–2024; season records refer to 2023. Each journey chapter links to its source.

The design

Inspired by the supplied Apple, Nike, ThoughtLab and Active Theory references. Barlow Condensed, Archivo and JetBrains Mono typography. Built with GSAP and an attributed Sketchfab helmet viewer.
